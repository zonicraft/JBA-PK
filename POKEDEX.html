How to read this

  • Chance is the share of encounters on that map and method. Grass and cave tables have 12 equally likely slots (8.3% each), surfing has 5 (20% each), and each rod picks evenly from its list. An encounter rolls roughly every 10 steps.
  • Water types only appear while surfing or fishing; everything else is found on land.
  • A Pokémon that evolves at Lv 24 is not found wild above Lv 23, and its evolution is not found below Lv 24.
  • Starters, legendaries and Mega forms never appear in the wild.
