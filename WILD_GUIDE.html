JBA Wild Guide

Where to find every Pokémon in the wild, with levels and the chance of meeting it on each encounter roll.

Maps are ordered by the level of their wild Pokémon, lowest first.

How to read this

  • Chance is the share of encounters on that map and method. Every slot in a table is equally likely: 12 slots for grass and caves (8.3% each), 5 for surfing (20% each), and each rod picks evenly from its list. An encounter rolls roughly every 10 steps on average.
  • Water-type Pokémon only appear while surfing or fishing. Every other Pokémon appears on land.
  • Evolutions respect levels: a Pokémon that evolves at Lv 24 is not found wild above Lv 23, and its evolution is not found below Lv 24.
  • Starters, legendaries and Mega forms never appear in the wild.
